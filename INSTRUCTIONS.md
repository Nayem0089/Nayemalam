# 📖 OPERATOR MANUAL & QUICK START GUIDE

Welcome to the **Esports Tournament Standings Manager**. Follow this guide to manage scrims, record match scores, configure points, and export graphics.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start in 60 Seconds

1. **Launch the Application:**
   - Double-click `run-server.bat` (or open `index.html` directly in your browser).
2. **Unlock Admin Privileges:**
   - Click the **🔒 Viewer Mode** button in the top navigation bar.
   - Enter the default password: **`admin123`** and click **Unlock Admin**.
   - The top banner turns green/gold and shows **Admin Mode Active**.
3. **Record Your First Match:**
   - In the right-side **Quick Point Update** panel:
     - **Select Squad:** Choose the team (e.g. `OutLaw`).
     - **Match #:** Enter `1` (or next match number).
     - **Position:** Select `1st Place (Booyah)` — Position Points automatically set to `12`.
     - **Kill Points:** Enter team kills (e.g. `14`).
   - Click the glowing red **SAVE MATCH RESULT** button.
   - Watch the leaderboard update instantly with rank animations and sound feedback!

---

## 🎮 How to Manage Groups

- **Switching Groups:** Click any group tab at the top (`GROUP A`, `GROUP B`, `GROUP C`, `GROUP D`). All scores and match histories are completely independent for each group.
- **Adding a New Group:** Click the dashed **+ Add Group** button. Enter the name (e.g. `GRAND FINALS`). You can choose to copy the squad names from your current group or start with fresh slots.
- **Renaming / Deleting Groups:** Go to **Settings (⚙) → Groups tab** to rename, reorder (◀ / ▶), duplicate, or delete groups.

---

## ⚡ Scoring Rules Configuration

To customize tournament point systems (e.g. PUBG Mobile, Free Fire World Series, or custom scrim rules):
1. Open **Settings (⚙)** from the top navigation bar.
2. Select the **Scoring** tab.
3. Configure:
   - **Kill Point Multiplier:** Default is `1.0` point per kill. Set to `2.0` for double-kill bonus events.
   - **Position Points Table:** Change the exact points awarded for 1st place through 16th place.
4. Changes automatically apply to all position dropdowns and recalculate standings.

---

## 📥 Exporting Standings for Social Media & Broadcasts

- **Download PNG (Highest Quality):**
  - Click the glowing red **"Download PNG"** button in the top navigation bar.
  - Generates a crystal-clear 2.5x high-resolution PNG image ready to post on Discord, Twitter/X, Instagram, and WhatsApp.
- **Fullscreen Broadcast Mode:**
  - Click the **⛶ Fullscreen** button.
  - Hides browser controls and provides a pristine 1080p/4K display for OBS Studio, vMix, or live stream overlays.
- **Export CSV / PDF:**
  - Use the buttons in the **Quick Actions** panel on the right side to download spreadsheet CSV files or printable PDF reports.

---

## 🔄 Undoing and Editing Mistakes

- **Undo Last Entry:** Click **Undo Last** (or press `Ctrl + Z`) to revert the latest match update.
- **Edit Past Matches:** In the **Match History** panel, click the pencil (✎) icon next to any match record to adjust kills or position. Team cumulative scores update automatically!
- **Delete Specific Match:** Click the `×` icon next to any record in Match History to delete it and roll back the points from that squad.

---

## 🎨 Changing Themes & Customizing Colors

1. Open **Settings (⚙) → Theme & Design** tab.
2. Choose one of the preset tournament themes:
   - **Red Esports (Default):** Warzone glowing red and gold accents.
   - **Gold Premium:** Luxe gold and titanium dark theme.
   - **Blue Gaming:** Cyberpunk electric cyan and cobalt blue.
   - **Purple Neon:** Synthwave neon violet.
3. Or use the **Custom Palette Pickers** to select any color code for your organization.
